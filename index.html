Local data & privacy

Typing scores and creature growth are stored in this browser's local storage, not on a server. Different devices and browser profiles do not synchronize. Private browsing, clearing browser data, or using a different computer may erase or hide progress. Nicknames are optional; avoid entering full student names.

Grade 5 and Grade 6 page practice supports sustained keyboarding similar to earlier WA/CCSS W.5.6 and W.6.6 expectations; it is not a formal standards assessment. Keystroke accuracy counts mistakes even if you correct them. Net WPM measures correctly matched characters divided by five and then by elapsed minutes. Corrections are encouraged and are not treated as a failed test.
